Welcome to Palestine Explorer

Palestine is a land rich in history, culture, and natural beauty. Explore its cities, traditions, and important places.

Explore the Governorates

Culture

Palestinian culture is rich in traditions, music, food, and hospitality. Common foods include maqluba, hummus, and falafel, and family gatherings are an important part of daily life.

Palestinian Governorates

Quds (Jerusalem)

Quds is known for its historic Old City, important religious sites, and rich cultural heritage.

Khaleel (Hebron)

Khaleel is a historic city known for traditional markets, old stone buildings, and local crafts.

Yafa (Jaffa)

Yafa is a historic coastal city celebrated for its old harbor, architecture, and Mediterranean setting.

Khan Younus

Khan Younus is a southern city with a long history, active communities, and strong cultural traditions.

Rafah

Rafah is a southern city known for its local history, community life, and location near the coast.

Gaza

Gaza is a coastal area with a long history, a strong cultural identity, and views of the Mediterranean Sea.
